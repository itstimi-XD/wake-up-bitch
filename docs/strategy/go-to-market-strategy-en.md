## 💰 Monetization Strategy

### 1. **Solo Mode (Free + Ads)**

**Model:**
- Core features completely free
- Banner ads (1-2 per day)
- Rewarded ads for temporary premium unlocks

**Revenue Projection:**
- 10,000 DAU baseline: $500-1,000/month (AdMob)
- eCPM: $1-2

**Benefits:**
- Low entry barrier
- Fast user acquisition
- Viral growth potential

### 2. **Party Mode (Freemium)**

**Free Features:**
- Add up to 3 friends
- 1 weekly challenge
- Basic leaderboard

**Premium Features ($2.99/month or $24.99/year):**
- Unlimited friends
- Unlimited challenges
- Custom mission creation
- Ad removal
- Premium themes/icons
- Advanced statistics

**Revenue Projection:**
- 5% conversion rate
- 10,000 DAU × 5% = 500 paid users
- Monthly revenue: $1,500

### 3. **Mission Packs (In-App Purchase)**

**Items for Sale:**
- **Boss Pack** ($0.99): 10 boss raid themes
- **Reality Pack** ($1.99): Advanced financial tracking
- **Voice Pack** ($0.99): Celebrity voice packs
- **Special Missions** ($0.99-2.99): Seasonal exclusive missions

**Revenue Projection:**
- 10% monthly purchase rate
- Average purchase: $1.50
- Monthly revenue: $1,500

### 💵 Total Projected Revenue (Monthly, 10K DAU)

| Revenue Source | Monthly Revenue | Share |

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Too Aggressive Branding
**Mitigation:**
- Enhance humor elements
- "Secret Mode" (family-friendly version)
- Adjustable text tone in settings

### Risk 2: Low Retention
**Mitigation:**
- Daily missions/rewards
- Streak gamification
- Optimized push notifications

### Risk 3: Technical Issues (Sensor malfunction)
**Mitigation:**
- Thorough device-specific testing
- Manual mode fallback
- Fast bug fix process

### Risk 4: Competitor Response
**Mitigation:**
- Differentiated missions (BILLS_DUE)
- Party Mode exclusivity
- Continuous updates
